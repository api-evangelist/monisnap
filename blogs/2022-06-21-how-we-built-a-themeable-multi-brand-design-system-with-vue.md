At Monisnap our goal is to enable users to support their friends and family abroad by providing remittance services where traditional banking services are unavailable. To this end, we build products under our own brand but we also have partners who leverage our expertise to enable their users to transfer money without the complexities that arise when dealing with multiple methods of international remittance. In 2021 the front-end team at Monisnap built the Monisnap Interface Kit ( Moniik for short).
