# MONI (Ex-Monisnap)

> MONI, formerly Monisnap, is a Paris-based remittance fintech founded in 2017 that lets
> diaspora customers in France, Spain and the United Kingdom send money to family in
> roughly 150 countries — as cash pickup, to mobile money wallets, as mobile airtime
> top-up, or as a prepaid card recharge. It operates a consumer app and web storefront
> and, alongside it, a B2B "Moni-as-a-Service" remittance-as-a-service platform that
> banks and neobanks (Nickel, Bnext) embed in their own applications. MONISNAP SAS is
> registered with the French ACPR as a payment services agent of MPS SAS, an authorised
> payment institution (CIB 17738).

## Agent note — no public API

MONI publishes **no public API**. As of 2026-08-17 there is no developer portal, no API
reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL contract, no MCP server and no A2A agent card on
any MONI host. `api.monisnap.com`, `docs.monisnap.com`, `developer.monisnap.com`,
`developers.monisnap.com` and `sandbox.monisnap.com` do not resolve in DNS. Every
`/.well-known/` discovery path on `www.monisnap.com` and `allo.monisnap.com` returns 404.
The remittance-as-a-service platform is reached through a partnership/sales conversation,
not a self-serve developer sign-up. Do not synthesise endpoints for MONI — there are none
to call.
